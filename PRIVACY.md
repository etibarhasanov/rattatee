# Rattatee privacy policy

*Last updated 23 November 2026.*

Rattatee is an iPhone app that records bike rides. It is published on the
App Store by Avida TECH OU, and it has no server of its own: everything it
sends goes to [tallinntastebuds.ee](https://tallinntastebuds.ee), whose
[privacy page](https://tallinntastebuds.ee/privacy) covers what that site
keeps. This page says what the app does.

For anything about your data, write to info@avidatech.eu.

## What stays on your phone

- **Your rides.** A ride is recorded on your phone and stays there until you
  save it or throw it away, and a saved ride stays in My rides on your phone.
- **How you moved.** When a ride is finished, the app asks the phone which
  parts of it you walked or went by car, and leaves those parts out. That
  answer never leaves the phone.
- **Your settings**, such as language, colours and the voice, and the places
  you last searched for. Deleting the app removes them.

## Location

The app asks for your location **While Using the App** when the map first
opens, and never asks for Always. It is used on your phone to show where you
are and to record a ride. While a ride is being recorded the app keeps
receiving your location with the screen locked, and iOS shows the blue
location indicator while it does. Nothing is recorded when you are not
riding. You can turn location off for the app in iPhone Settings at any time;
the app then cannot record a ride.

## What is sent, and when

- **Searching for a place.** What you type, and where your phone is, are sent
  to tallinntastebuds.ee to find places and how far they are. They are not
  kept with anything that says who asked.
- **Asking for the way somewhere.** Where you are and where you are going are
  sent to tallinntastebuds.ee, which passes a rounded position to the routing
  service it uses (OpenStreetMap Germany) to work out the way by bike. They
  are not kept and not connected to your account.
- **Saving a ride without an account.** When you press Save, the parts of
  the ride inside Tallinn, Tartu, Pärnu and Narva are sent. The site adds
  them to its counts of how each stretch of street is ridden — how many
  rides crossed it, which way and how steadily — and **keeps nothing of the
  ride itself**. The ride stays on your phone.
- **Saving a ride with an account.** The site keeps the ride under your
  account: the way you rode with the first and last 200 metres taken off,
  so where you start and stop is not kept; its length, times and name; and
  the numbers of the whole ride, such as distance, riding time and top
  speed, which it adds up into totals only you can see. Anyone can ask the
  site for the way of a ride, but never with your username or the ride's
  name.

## Accounts

You can use Rattatee without an account. If you make one, it is a username
and a password, or Sign in with Apple or Google, and it is the same account
as on tallinntastebuds.ee. With Apple or Google the site keeps the number
Apple or Google gives for you — never your email address or your name.

**Delete account**, under Account in the app, removes your account, every
way into it, and the numbers and totals of your rides at once. The ways of
rides you saved stay, with no account left for them to belong to, and so
does what they added to the counts of the streets. To have those ways
removed too, write to info@avidatech.eu.

## What the app does not do

- No analytics, no advertising and no third-party code in the app.
- No tracking across other apps or websites.
- Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

The map is drawn by Apple Maps, so Apple receives your phone's internet
address in order to send the map.

## Changes

If what the app sends or keeps changes, this page changes with it, and the
date at the top says when.
